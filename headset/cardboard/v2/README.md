# Cardboard headset v2: double face ring

Draft. `python3 gen.py --v2` writes this folder; `python3 laser.py --v2` writes `laser/`. v1 (the parent folder, the picture guide) is unchanged.

## What changed from v1

| | v1 | v2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face ring | R, one layer | **R1 (back) + R2 (face)**, hot-glued into one double layer |
| Wrap tabs on the ring | none | **8 per layer:** 2 top, 2 bottom, 2 on each side. They fold back over A, B, SC and SU and get glued down. R2's tabs fold one card further out, so they wrap over R1's. |
| Strap ears | 16 mm deep, 42 mm long | 22 mm deep, 50 mm long: 9 mm of card around each strap slot instead of 6, and two layers |
| Body tabs into the ring (A, B, SC, SU) | 3 mm long, flush with R | **14 mm:** through both layers, plus 8 mm sticking out to fold flat and tape or glue |
| Tab widths (= slot lengths in R) | A 16 + 12 + 16, B 16 + 16, sides 20 | A 12 + 16 + 12, **B 24 + 24, sides 30**. A's end tabs move to 2–14 mm from the ends, to leave room for the top wrap tabs |
| Slot width in R | 3.3 mm (0.3 clearance) | **3.5 mm** (0.5 clearance): each slot goes through two glued layers, which never line up perfectly |
| F's band hooks | one layer | **FH × 4:** doublers glued onto the part of each hook outside the body |

## Assembly changes (in v1's step 5)

1. **Glue R1 to R2:** printed sides facing the same way (toward the face), slots lined up. Push a scrap tab through two slots to line them up while the glue sets. If you can, cut R1 and R2 with the card's flutes running at right angles to each other: the pair is much stiffer.
2. **Body into the ring:** push A's, B's, SC's and SU's tabs through both layers. Fold the 8 mm that sticks out flat against R2 and tape or glue it. The face foam covers them.
3. **Wrap tabs:** fold each of R1's tabs back along the wall it sits on and glue it down. Then fold R2's tab over it and glue it on top.
4. **FH:** glue one doubler onto each of F's four hooks, on the part that sticks out past A or B, flap side, with the notches lined up. The rubber band then pulls on two layers.

R2 adds 3 mm between the face and the lenses. If the view gets narrower, use slightly thinner face foam.
